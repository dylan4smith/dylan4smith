How to Play

🛒

1. Shop for Monsters

Each floor, spend 💰 gold to recruit Cryptid monsters from the market. Sell unwanted ones for 1💰 or reroll the shop for 2💰.

⭐

2. Merge to Evolve

Collect 3 of the same monster to automatically merge them into a stronger version with a ⭐ tier bonus. Higher tiers have massively increased stats!

⚔️

3. Watch Auto-Battles

Your monsters fight automatically based on Speed and abilities. The order matters — your team fights from front to back. Use the battle log to understand what happened.

🃏

4. Draft Upgrades

Win a battle and choose one of three upgrade cards. Cards can boost all monsters, grant new abilities, or change the rules of combat. Choose wisely!

📖

5. Build the Codex

Earn ✨ Essence from every run. Spend it in the Codex to permanently unlock new advantages that carry over between runs.

⚡ Elements: Fire beats Nature, Water beats Fire, Electric beats Water, Nature beats Electric. Dark and Light counter each other. Use elemental matchups for 40% bonus damage!
🎯 Goal: Clear all 15 floors and defeat the final boss. Bosses appear on floors 5, 10, and 15. Every run is different!
